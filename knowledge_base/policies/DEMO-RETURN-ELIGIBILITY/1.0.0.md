# Synthetic demo rule

This is fictional training content for the development fixture. It does not state a real marketplace return window or entitlement.

## Handling

Do not answer yes or no from product category alone. First verify the authorized order item, marketplace, item attributes, purchase date, and an approved policy version in scope for that item and date. If any required evidence is missing, return insufficient evidence and route the case for human review.

Do not treat a ticket label, generated fixture field, search score, or model output as a policy decision.

## When an order item is reported damaged

Use this synthetic workflow when a customer says an item arrived damaged, broken, or defective. Confirm the caller is authorized to discuss the order and identify the specific order item. Record the customer's description as a report; do not treat a delivery scan, product category, or the fixture's condition field as proof that damage did or did not occur. Request only the evidence required by the approved policy that applies to the marketplace, item, and purchase date. If that policy or required evidence is unavailable, explain that eligibility cannot be confirmed from the available information and route the case for human review. Do not promise or initiate a return, replacement, refund, or compensation from this draft guidance.
